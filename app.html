BandCode

Read a resistor's color bands, or find the bands for the value you need.

About this

Digits black 0, brown 1, red 2, orange 3, yellow 4, green 5, blue 6, violet 7, grey 8, white 9. The multiplier band is a power of ten (gold x0.1, silver x0.01). Gold is 5% and silver 10%; a 5-band resistor with a brown last band is usually 1%. Worked example from Wikipedia: red red blue brown brown = 2.26 kΩ at 1%. Which end is the start is not always clear on a real part; the tolerance band is usually set apart or gold or silver. Measure with a meter when it matters. Runs in your browser.
